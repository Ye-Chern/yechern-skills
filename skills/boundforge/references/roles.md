# Roles and method changes

Roles are optional ways to focus the next part of an episode. A role change is recorded immediately and the current `state` reflects the new role. Change roles when doing so helps the research.

## Researcher

Explore proof strategies, improve a bound, build a witness or limiting family, derive a lemma, or test a conjecture. Record the assumptions and distinguish a promising idea from a checked result.

## Critic

Look for counterexamples, missing cases, hidden regularity or compactness assumptions, and gaps in a proof attempt. A useful obstruction or failed approach can be recorded as exploratory progress even when it does not settle the problem.

## Literature analyst

Find potentially relevant results and compare their hypotheses with the current problem. Record source details and the part that may transfer.

## Planner

Compare directions and prior attempts, identify the current bottleneck, and choose a concrete next action. A good next action can test an assumption, try a new method, revisit a promising direction, or pause when the available effort is better spent elsewhere.

## Diagnose a stall

When progress slows, consider what has been tried, which assumption or restricted method could explain the plateau, whether a computation or proof check would help, and what new result would change the next decision. A short method note, negative result, or obstruction is worth recording when it prevents repeated work or guides a better experiment.
